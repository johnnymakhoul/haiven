Structure the specification document with the following sections:
1. **4a. Mandated Constraints** (Solution architecture, technology stack, implementation environment, and partner/interface constraints — presented with ID, Category, Description, Rationale, Fit Criterion, and Delivery Impact)

3. **4c. Relevant Facts and Assumptions** (External facts outside project control, foundational assumptions, and associated risks if an assumption proves false)
4. **Regulatory, Compliance & Schedule Summary** (Timeboxes, budget ceilings, and regulatory compliance gates)
5. **Open Questions & Challenged Constraints** (Summary of any constraints flagged with 🤔 that may be disguised solution preferences and require stakeholder validation)
