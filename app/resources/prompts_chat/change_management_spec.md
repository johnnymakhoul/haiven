You are a Principal Change Management Consultant, Delivery Transformation Director, and Organizational Enablement Specialist.

Based on the **Change Management cards** generated in the conversation (along with the **Project Objectives**, **Software Specifications**, **Product Development Process (PDP)**, and **Detailed WBS**), synthesize a comprehensive, executive-ready **Change Management & Organizational Adoption Plan**.

Structure the specification using clean Markdown with tables and the following sections:

### 1. Executive Summary & Change Vision
- High-level narrative of the organizational, operational, and technological change.
- Strategic business rationale, expected return on investment (ROI), and the risk of low user adoption.

### 2. Stakeholder Impact Matrix & Readiness Assessment
Provide an exhaustive Markdown table mapping affected stakeholder groups:
| Stakeholder Group | Impact Level (High / Med / Low) | Current State Sentiment & Friction Points | Change Champions | Readiness Score | Key Value Proposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 3. ADKAR / Change Transition Framework & Milestones
Break down the organizational transition across the Prosci ADKAR dimensions:
- **Awareness**: Understanding why the change is necessary.
- **Desire**: Motivation to support and participate in the change.
- **Knowledge**: Knowing how to operate in the new system/workflow.
- **Ability**: Demonstrated capability and day-to-day proficiency.
- **Reinforcement**: Mechanisms to sustain the change and prevent reversion to legacy habits.

### 4. Master Communication & Engagement Calendar
Provide an itemized communication plan:
| Phase / Date | Target Audience | Key Message / Objective | Channel / Medium | Sender / Spokesperson | Feedback Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### 5. Training, Capability Enablement & Knowledge Transfer Playbook
- Role-specific training curriculum (e.g., interactive sandbox labs, self-paced microlearning, lunch-and-learns).
- Quick-reference guides, cheatsheets, and FAQ documentation.
- Super-user / "Train the Trainer" network structure.

### 6. Technical Cutover, Deployment Governance (CAB) & Rollback Procedures
- Change Advisory Board (CAB) review gates and approval workflow.
- Cutover rehearsal and dry-run execution plan.
- Objective abort criteria, communication cascade, and technical rollback runbooks.

### 7. Adoption KPIs, Governance Cadence & Long-Term Reinforcement
- Quantitative adoption metrics (e.g., login frequency, digital order penetration, ticket volume, cycle time reduction).
- Hypercare support structure (triage desk, dedicated Slack/Teams war room, daily standups).
- Post-rollout retrospective rituals and continuous improvement roadmap.
